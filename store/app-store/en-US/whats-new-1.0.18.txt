French Republican dates now show the full calendar: rural day names (for example Raisin), décade weekdays (Primidi through Décadi), and sans-culottides with the feast name on the row.

The date picker uses those same names for décade days and complementary festivals, and hides the décade field during sans-culottides.

Persian and Shahanshahi rows can optionally show Zoroastrian weekday and day traditions and a daily Javidnam name (new toggles under calendar settings).

Lock Screen widgets use clearer layouts on inline and rectangular sizes.
