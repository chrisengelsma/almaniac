See the same date in many calendars at once, in their native scripts.

Almaniac is a multi-calendar converter that shows Gregorian, Julian, Hebrew, Islamic, Persian, Indian, French Republican, Maya, and Julian Day dates on a single screen.

ALL CALENDARS AT ONCE
Pick a date and see every calendar system side by side. No back-and-forth converting between source and target.

NATIVE SCRIPTS
Dates appear in Arabic, Hebrew, and Devanagari with proper fonts, not just romanized text. Toggle transliteration when you prefer English month names.

RELIGIOUS HOLIDAYS
Major Christian, Jewish, and Islamic holidays appear on the dates they fall. Toggle each tradition on or off in settings.

ANCIENT DATES MADE EASY
Jump to any year by typing it, including BCE dates for historical research. No endless scrolling to reach 100 BCE.

CALENDAR INFO
Tap any calendar for its history, solar/lunar type, and an interactive map of where it's used today.

BUILT FOR PRIVACY
No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Works fully offline.

Also includes Islamic calendar day adjustment, drag-to-reorder rows, copy-to-clipboard, and home screen widgets.
